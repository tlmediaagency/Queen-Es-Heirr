Support for Program Staff and Advisors

  • J.U.D.A.H. for Professionals gives advisors, coordinators and community leaders a leadership model to use with their own teams.
  • The J.U.D.A.H. Leadership keynote works for student gatherings, staff development days and conferences.

Who We Serve

TRIO programs such as Upward Bound, Talent Search and Student Support Services, GEAR UP, college-access and mentoring programs, schools and districts, universities and student organizations, churches and faith-based youth ministries, and community nonprofits. We work with secular and faith-based organizations alike, anywhere in the United States.

How to Get Started

  1. Tell us about your students. Share your program, the age group, your goals and your timing in the Ask About a Program form.
  2. We recommend a fit. A curriculum, an academy, a workshop or a keynote.
  3. We plan it with you. We follow up to confirm the details.

Questions Educators Ask

What leadership programs does Queen E's Heirr offer for TRIO, GEAR UP and other college-access programs?

Queen E's Heirr offers J.U.D.A.H. for Students, a student leadership curriculum, along with the Student Leadership Academy and Youth Dining & Social Graces. A J.U.D.A.H. leadership keynote is also available for students, staff and conferences. Programs can be tailored to your group, so tell us about your students and goals.

Do you serve programs outside Louisiana?

Yes. Queen E's Heirr serves schools and organizations nationwide. Tell us where your group is and how you would like to meet, and we will plan it with you.

Who leads the sessions?

Sessions are led by LyKinda Thibodeaux, the author of the J.U.D.A.H. Leadership Model, and Queen E's facilitators.

What age groups do you work with?

Youth Dining & Social Graces is designed for ages 8 to 17. The Student Leadership Academy and J.U.D.A.H. for Students serve students, and the professional curriculum serves the staff and leaders who guide them. Ask us which fits your group.

Can you speak to our staff or at our conference?

Yes. The J.U.D.A.H. Leadership keynote presents the curriculum as a talk for schools, programs and organizations, and the professional edition supports the staff who lead student programs.

How do we get started?

Use the Ask About a Program form on the Etiquette & Leadership page. Share your program, student group, goals and timing, and we will recommend a fit.
