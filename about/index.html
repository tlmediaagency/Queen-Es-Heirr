Our story

Born From Passion, Preserved for Generations

Queen E's Heirr (say it "Queenie's") is a family business. Every jar of jam and every pickled delicacy is made by hand in small batches in Slidell, Louisiana, from recipes and traditions passed down through four generations of women.

Shop the jars Join a class

Meet the maker

LyKinda Thibodeaux

LyKinda is the founder, cook and teacher behind Queen E's Heirr. Canning and preserving came to her the way the best traditions do: from her grandmother, Essie, to her mother, Queen Esther, and now on to her daughter, Jaleesa. For LyKinda, it was never only a hobby. It was a way to stay close to her heritage and keep what her family built alive.

Before the jam, she spent more than 20 years in Human Resources, leading teams and managing the details of large projects at companies including Chevron, Heart of Florida United Way and Nestle Health Science. She studied sociology at The University of Southern Mississippi. Those years taught her how people connect, communicate and make each other feel welcome, and that is the same spirit she brings to every jar, every class and every table.

Etiquette & social skills

Navigate Any Social Situation With Grace

Imagine navigating any social situation with grace and ease, leaving a lasting positive impression, and building meaningful connections effortlessly. That's the power of mastering etiquette and social skills, and LyKinda Thibodeaux, your expert guide, has helped individuals and organizations thrive for over 20 years.

LyKinda isn't just an instructor; she's a seasoned professional with over 20 years in Human Resources, where she honed her expertise in human interaction, communication, and building strong relationships in diverse settings. From leading teams at Chevron to managing logistics for multi-million dollar projects, she understands the nuances of professional interaction and communication. Her time at Heart of Florida United Way and Nestle Health Science further solidified her passion for service and community, which shines through in her teaching and commitment to Kingdom principles.

LyKinda graduated with a Bachelor's degree in Liberal Studies - Sociology from The University of Southern Mississippi. This foundation, combined with her diverse work experience, allows her to connect with individuals from all walks of life and tailor her instruction to their specific needs.

LyKinda believes true etiquette goes beyond superficial rules. She seamlessly weaves Kingdom principles into her curriculum, fostering genuine kindness, consideration, and respect, the foundations of truly meaningful connections. This unique approach sets her apart, empowering individuals and organizations to navigate the social landscape with confidence and integrity.

Whether you're a young professional seeking career-boosting social skills, or an individual longing to forge stronger personal connections, LyKinda's program offerings cater to your specific needs. From the fundamental introductions to advanced dining etiquette, she equips you with the tools and techniques to excel in any environment.

Small business

A Woman-Owned, Family-Owned Small Business

Queen E's Heirr is a woman-owned, family-owned small business in Slidell, Louisiana. Founded in 2022 by LyKinda Thibodeaux, it is made by hand in small batches by a family, not a factory. When you buy a jar, book a class or bring in leadership training, you are supporting a local, women-led business and the next generation of a family tradition.

We welcome retail and wholesale partners, companies and HR teams, and schools and youth programs who want to work with a small, woman-owned business.

Our mission

Quality Products Every Time

At Queen E's, we're more than just makers of delicious jams and pickles. We're a family guided by faith, sharing God's bounty one spoonful at a time. We pour our hearts, along with the freshest, natural ingredients, into every jar, believing that good food feeds not just the body, but the soul.

Our mission isn't just about flavor, it's about feeling. We want you to taste the sunshine in our summer berries, the earthiness of our homegrown vegetables, and the love that goes into every recipe, passed down through generations.

Choosing Queen E's is more than just a purchase, it's a connection. You're supporting a small business built on faith and family values, and joining a community that believes in sharing the simple joys of good food.

So come gather around our table, virtually or in person. Savor the flavors, feel the love, and experience the blessing that comes from sharing homemade goodness.

How it began

From One Jar to a Whole Pantry

Generations

Essie and Queen Esther

Two women who canned, preserved and shared. The business carries their names: Queen E's began as "Queen Essie's," and their names are part of every label.

June 2022

A Jar at a Produce Stand

It was not a plan. A longtime friend who owned a local produce stand asked LyKinda for pickled cucumbers. She made a batch of those and a jar of jam. Both sold out within days.

Today

Jams, Pickles and Classes

Queen E's now ships across the United States, pops up at events and markets, sits on shelves at Bayou Stuf in Lacombe, and teaches hands-on jam-making classes.

Four generations

The Heart and Soul of Queen E's

Essie, Queen Esther, LyKinda and Jaleesa: four generations, one family table. When you open a jar, you are tasting recipes that were loved long before there was a label on them.

"We pour our hearts into every jar, using the freshest ingredients and time-tested recipes passed down through generations. We hope you can taste the love and dedication in every bite."
LyKinda

In every jar

Small Batch, Fresh Fruit, Southern Flavor

  • Made by hand in small batches in Slidell, Louisiana, on the Northshore of Lake Pontchartrain.
  • Fresh, seasonal fruit and ingredients sourced as close to home as possible. Flavors come and go with the season.
  • Southern and Louisiana-inspired flavors such as peppered jam, jalapeño pepper jam, pineapple mango pepper jam and blueberry ginger, alongside classics like strawberry, peach and blackberry.
  • Pickled goods that wear the same Queen E label, from dill to candied jalapeños.

More Than Jam

LyKinda believes etiquette is not about stiff rules. It is about kindness, consideration and respect, grounded in the Kingdom principles she lives by. She and her facilitators teach that through hands-on jam-making classes, etiquette and leadership programs, and the J.U.D.A.H. leadership curriculum for students, professionals and organizations.

Questions People Ask

How do you say "Queen E's"?

"Queenie's." The name began as "Queen Essie's," in honor of LyKinda's grandmother, Essie, and her mother, Queen Esther.

Where is Queen E's Heirr based?

Slidell, Louisiana, on the Northshore of Lake Pontchartrain in St. Tammany Parish. The jars ship across the United States and are sold at events, pop-ups and in store at Bayou Stuf in Lacombe.

Who makes the jams?

LyKinda Thibodeaux makes them by hand, in small batches, with help from her family.

Is the jam really handmade?

Yes. Every jar is cooked by hand in small batches from family recipes. That is why some flavors sell out and why others return only when the fruit is in season.
