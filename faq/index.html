Questions

Good Things to Know

Are the jams small batch?

Yes. LyKinda makes them in small batches from family recipes, with fresh fruit and ingredients that are mostly local.

Where is Queen E's Heirr made?

Queen E's Heirr jams and pickled goods are made by hand in Slidell, Louisiana, on the Northshore of Lake Pontchartrain, and are sold online, at events and pop-ups, and in store at Bayou Stuf in Lacombe, Louisiana.

Is Queen E's an artisan or handmade jam?

Yes. Every jar is cooked in small batches by hand from family recipes, using fresh fruit and ingredients sourced as close to home as possible. Flavors change with the seasons, so jars are made when the fruit is at its best rather than on a factory schedule.

What makes Queen E's different from store-bought jam?

Queen E's is made in small batches with fresh seasonal fruit. The flavors lean Southern and Louisiana-inspired, including peppered jam, jalapeño pepper jam, pineapple mango pepper jam and blueberry ginger, alongside classics like strawberry, peach and blackberry. Many jars pair with cheese, charcuterie and grilling as well as toast.

What are the best Queen E's jams for a charcuterie board or gift?

Peppered Jam, Blueberry Peppered Jam, Pineapple Mango Pepper Jam and Jalapeño Pepper Jam are favorites for cheese and charcuterie boards. Peach, Raspberry and Strawberry Mango make easy, crowd-pleasing gifts. Pickled goods such as Poppin Sweet Peppers and the dill and tart pickles round out a board.

Are all flavors available year-round?

Most of our flavors are seasonal and may not be available year-round. Queen E's provides the freshest ingredients possible, so some flavors are only made when the fruit is in season. If a flavor is sold out, check back soon or join the mailing list to hear when it returns.

What sizes do the jars come in?

Jams come in Regular (8 oz) and Large (12 oz), plus a 4 oz specialty jar for sampling or gifting. Pickled goods come in Regular (12 oz) and Large (16 oz). Not every flavor is offered in every size.

Do the pickles use the same jars?

Yes. Pickled vegetables wear the same Queen E label as the jams.

How long do jars keep?

Unopened, store them cool and dark for up to 18 months. After opening, refrigerate and enjoy within 4 to 6 weeks.

Do you ship?

Jars ship across the United States at USPS rates calculated from your ZIP code in the bag. Orders are made in small batches and take about 5 business days to prepare, plus USPS delivery time. You can also order online and collect it at an upcoming event or pop-up we post, or find jars in store at Bayou Stuf in Lacombe, Louisiana.

Can I book a jam making or etiquette class?

Yes. Queen E's hosts hands-on jam making and STEM labs and combined jam making and etiquette classes, and takes private group requests. See the Classes page for upcoming dates and to book and pay online.

What is J.U.D.A.H.?

It is Queen E's leadership curriculum for students and professionals, and it is also offered as a keynote.

Can Queen E's come to my festival, market or event?

Yes. Use the Invite Queen E's page to tell us about your pop-up, festival, market or private event.

Can I buy Queen E's jam wholesale to sell in my store or use in my restaurant?

Yes. Stores, restaurants and other businesses can buy Queen E's at a wholesale discount. Use the Wholesale page to choose the jars you would like and we will send you a quote to approve. Once you are an approved vendor, you can reorder any time in the Wholesale Portal.

How do stores carry Queen E's?

Use the Wholesale page to choose the jars you would like. Wholesale customers receive a discount on every jar, and we send you a quote to approve. Stores can also use the form on Locations.

How do I reorder as a current wholesale vendor?

Approved vendors can sign in to the Wholesale Portal with a code sent to their email, send a refill request and get an instant quote. LyKinda reviews it, adjusts quantities if needed and sets a drop-off date, then you approve the final quote. You can pay by card online before the drop-off or by check at drop-off.

Do wholesale orders include sales tax?

Stores that resell Queen E's jars need to give us a resale certificate. Businesses that use the product in a restaurant or service may be taxed differently. Ask us if you are not sure which applies.
