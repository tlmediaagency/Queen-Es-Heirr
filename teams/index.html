Why Queen E's Heirr

The J.U.D.A.H. Leadership Model was written by LyKinda Thibodeaux, who spent more than 20 years in Human Resources, including leadership roles at Chevron, Heart of Florida United Way and Nestle Health Science. She knows what happens when teams communicate well, and what it costs when they do not. Sessions are led by LyKinda and our facilitators, with a practical, warm approach: leadership and etiquette as a way of making people feel respected, not as stiff rules. Read her story.

  3. We plan it together. We follow up to confirm the details with you.

Questions Leaders Ask

What leadership training does Queen E's Heirr offer for teams and companies?

Queen E's Heirr offers the J.U.D.A.H. Leadership Model and curriculum for professionals, an Executive Presence & Dining program, and a J.U.D.A.H. leadership keynote for schools and companies. The J.U.D.A.H. Leadership Model book and curriculum resources are also available for leaders to use with their own teams.

Who is the training for, and where do you serve?

Executive managers, HR and people leaders, nonprofit and community leaders, and the people who lead student and youth programs, including secular and faith-based organizations. We serve organizations nationwide.

Can Queen E's speak at our company or school event?

Yes. The J.U.D.A.H. Leadership keynote presents the curriculum as a talk for schools, companies and organizations anywhere in the United States. Tell us about your event and audience and we will follow up.

Do you offer team-building or private group sessions?

Yes. Queen E's takes private group requests for hands-on jam-making classes and combined jam-making and etiquette classes, which many groups use as a memorable team experience.

How do I ask about a program for my organization?

Use the Ask About a Program form on the Etiquette & Leadership page, or the Invite Queen E's page for events, and share your group, goals and timing. We will recommend a fit.
