Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our confections, shipping, classes, and wholesale accounts.

Are your jams and confections made in small batches?

Yes! Every single jar of preserves and pickled delicacy is meticulously crafted in small batches using pristine vine-ripened fruits and traditional family recipes to ensure exceptional flavor and quality.

How do the Jam-Making & STEM Etiquette Classes work?

We offer both standalone jam-making & STEM science labs (exploring fruit pectin and thermodynamics) and combined classes that pair the science session with formal tea service and etiquette training.

Can I book private etiquette or leadership coaching for my team?

Absolutely! We offer customized corporate leadership training, youth cotillion prep, and private workshops tailored specifically to your organization or event requirements.

What is the shelf life of your artisanal jams once opened?

Unopened jars can be stored in a cool, dark place for up to 18 months. Once opened, keep refrigerated and enjoy within 4 to 6 weeks for optimal freshness and vibrant flavor.

How can retail stores stock Queen E's products?

Boutiques and retail stockists can apply through our Wholesale Portal tab. Once submitted, our team will review your reseller details and send you direct wholesale case pricing and catalog information.

Do you ship orders nationwide?

Yes! We securely package and ship our gourmet preserves and pickled goods directly to your doorstep across the United States. Local pickup is also available at our featured partner Bayou Stuf in Lacombe, LA.

What should I bring to a Jam-Making STEM Lab?

All culinary equipment, aprons, science materials, and ingredients are provided. Just bring your curiosity and excitement for hands-on learning!

Can I request a custom flavor or gift basket?

Yes! For weddings, corporate gifting, or private events, we create custom preserve collections and curated gift boxes. Contact us through our inquiry forms to discuss your vision.
