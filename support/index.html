Questions about an order, a jar, a class, or anything else? Send us a note and we will reply by email.

Damaged or incorrect order

Email us within 7 days of delivery with your order details and a photo, and we'll make it right. Because these are food products, we can't accept returns of opened jars.

Shipping

We ship within the United States. Delivery times are estimates, and jars are glass, so please check your package on arrival. Shipping and any tax are shown at checkout before you pay.

Allergens and ingredients

Check the jar label for ingredients and storage instructions. If you have an allergy question, contact us before ordering.
